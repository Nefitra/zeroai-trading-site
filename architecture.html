public boundary

What we disclose — and what we do not.

The goal is scientific transparency without publishing the recipe for reproducing or attacking the system.

Safe to disclose

  • Research stages, experiment IDs and high-level study purpose.
  • Evidence boundaries: TRAIN, Fresh Validation, Historical TEST and deployment.
  • Frozen status, aggregate structural counts and CI pass/fail state.
  • Whether selection, TEST access or live trading is authorized.
  • High-level causality, preregistration and fail-closed methodology.

Kept private

  • Exact feature-generation recipes, transformations and proprietary signals.
  • Internal model weights, checkpoints, optimizer state and training artifacts.
  • Detailed execution calibration, private risk tuning and operational parameters.
  • Private repository structure beyond what is already intentionally published.
  • Credentials, infrastructure topology, deployment endpoints and exchange access.
scientific controls

The public map emphasizes gates, not alpha.

Causality gate

Information enters the model only when economically available.

Freeze gate

Treatments, candidates and decision rules are locked before Fresh reveal.

Reveal gate

Partial Fresh performance has no selection authority.

Deployment gate

Research success does not automatically authorize live capital.

Security posture: architecture shown here is intentionally incomplete. Missing implementation detail is a design choice, not missing documentation. The public site should explain scientific integrity without exposing operational or proprietary internals.
