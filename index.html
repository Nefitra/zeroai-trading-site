research principles

Built to resist self-deception.

The objective is not to manufacture an attractive backtest. The objective is to maintain a research process where assumptions, failures and authorization boundaries remain visible.

[01]

Causality first

Information enters observations only after it is economically available.

[02]

Frozen decisions

Treatments, windows, candidates and selection rules are specified before reveal.

[03]

Real friction

Fees, spread, slippage, funding and risk mechanics stay inside the economics.

[04]

Failures remain visible

Closed studies and ineligible candidates remain part of the scientific record.
