methodology

Built to resist self-deception.

The objective is not to manufacture an attractive backtest. It is to create a research pipeline where economic assumptions, authorization gates and failures remain visible.

[01]

Causal data only

Higher-timeframe values are aligned only after their information is actually available.

[02]

Validation is isolated

Frozen validation data is separated from learning and historical TEST access.

[03]

Real friction

Fees, spread, slippage and funding are modeled explicitly instead of hidden behind headline PnL.

[04]

Failed runs stay visible

EXP-0004 failed validation and remains part of project history rather than being quietly discarded.
