hypothesis

Require repeated directional intent before entry.

The study asks whether a small number of consecutive identical directional requests can increase entry selectivity and reduce execution drag without changing directional symmetry, costs, leverage, sizing, risk limits, reward coefficients, market features, PPO hyperparameters or the 30-minute decision cadence.

arms

state machine

Pending intent is Markov-visible.

While FLAT

FLAT cancels pending confirmation. A new LONG/SHORT starts count 1. The same direction increments the count. Opposite direction replaces pending direction and resets count to 1. Until threshold is reached, effective action remains FLAT.

While positioned

Same-position HOLD is preserved. Voluntary FLAT exit is immediate. In treatment arms, an opposite-direction request first closes to FLAT and begins pending confirmation for the opposite direction. Forced risk close always has priority.
